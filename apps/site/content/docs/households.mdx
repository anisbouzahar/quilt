title: Households and currencies
description: Shared workspaces, clear permissions, and original monetary values.

## A workspace represents a household

A user can belong to multiple workspaces. Financial accounts, transactions,
merchants, categories, tags, budgets, goals, and supporting financial records
belong to a workspace.

A financial account is separate from a user identity. The current proposal
shares financial accounts within a workspace; private accounts within a
household are not modeled yet.

## Membership roles

| Role   | Read shared finances | Edit shared finances | Manage membership |
| ------ | -------------------- | -------------------- | ----------------- |
| Owner  | Yes                  | Yes                  | Yes               |
| Editor | Yes                  | Yes                  | No                |
| Viewer | Yes                  | No                   | No                |

These roles are confirmed product decisions, represented by a tested pure
permission policy. The server must still load current membership, enforce
permissions on every request, and preserve at least one owner. Authentication,
invitations, and membership endpoints have not been implemented.

## Accounts keep their currencies

A household may hold accounts in different currencies. A workspace’s preferred
reporting currency does not replace those original amounts.

For example, EUR 100 and USD 100 are two distinct quantities. The domain package
rejects adding them without explicit conversion. Its spending helper returns
separate totals per currency rather than inventing a combined balance.

## Explicit conversions

Conversion records retain the original amount, the converted amount, the rate,
its source and dates, and the chosen precision and rounding mode.

A production FX source, the initial supported currencies, and the rules for
historical versus current valuation are still open decisions. The existing
currency-code constructor validates syntax, not an approved currency registry.

## Online editing and cached viewing

The agreed direction requires a connection to submit edits. Cached data may be
viewable offline, but the interface must make its age and coverage clear.

The prototype does not yet implement this cache. A partial cached transaction
list must not be presented as a complete household balance.
