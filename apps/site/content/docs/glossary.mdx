---
title: Glossary
description: The shared language for Quilt households and financial activity.
---

Quilt brings a household’s spending, monthly plans, and yearly goals into a
shared picture of its money.

## People and ownership

**User**:
A person who can belong to one or more workspaces.
_Avoid_: Account when referring to a person’s identity.

**Workspace**:
A shared boundary for one household’s financial records and membership.
_Avoid_: Account, group.

**Membership**:
A user’s access to a workspace as an owner, editor, or viewer. An owner manages
membership, owners and editors edit shared finances, and viewers only read.

**Financial account**:
A workspace’s record of money held or owed, denominated in one currency.
_Avoid_: User account, workspace balance.

## Actual activity

**Money**:
An exact amount together with its currency. Amounts in different currencies
are distinct financial quantities until explicitly converted.

**Transaction**:
An actual or pending financial movement classified as an expense, income,
refund, or transfer.
_Avoid_: Bill, receipt, contribution when describing all transactions.

**Allocation**:
A portion of an expense, income, or refund assigned to a category or left
uncategorized. Allocations together account for the entire transaction amount.
_Avoid_: Split when referring to who owes a share of an expense.

**Refund**:
Money returned for an expense, optionally linked to its original transaction.
It reduces expense spending rather than becoming ordinary income.

**Transfer**:
One movement between two distinct accounts in the same workspace, retaining
both amounts when currencies differ. It is neither household income nor spending.

**Merchant**:
A user-managed business or counterparty associated with a transaction.

**Category**:
A user-managed classification for income or expense allocations.
_Avoid_: Tag when describing the classification used for a budget.

**Tag**:
An optional label used to organize transactions independently of categories.

**Conversion quote**:
A dated, attributed rate for converting one currency into another. It does not
replace the original amount or establish that a transfer executed at that rate.

## Plans and commitments

**Budget**:
A household’s spending plan for one calendar month, expressed in a planning
currency and divided into category limits.
_Avoid_: Account balance, forecast.

**Goal**:
A household’s target amount for a calendar year, measured in a goal currency.
_Avoid_: Account balance.

**Goal contribution**:
An allocation of progress toward a goal, optionally linked to an existing
transaction. It does not move money by itself.

**Recurring bill**:
A monthly payment expectation that produces dated bill occurrences.
_Avoid_: Transaction when describing a payment expectation.

**Bill occurrence**:
One dated expectation from a recurring bill. Actual payments are linked
transactions, not additional spending created by the occurrence itself.

## Evidence and intake

**Import batch**:
One attempt to bring source financial records into a workspace.

**Import row**:
One source record in an import batch, retaining its identity and any decision
to create, match, or ignore a transaction.

**Receipt attachment**:
A private document associated with a workspace, optionally linked to a
transaction. A receipt or invoice alone does not establish that payment occurred.

**Extraction result**:
The original facts and uncertainty returned by document processing, separate
from a person’s corrections.

**Expense draft**:
A reviewable proposal for an expense, potentially incomplete and supported by
a receipt. It becomes a transaction only through an explicit acceptance decision.
