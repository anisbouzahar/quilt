Quilt is being built for couples and households who want to understand their
spending, make monthly plans, and work toward longer-term goals together.

## Where the project is today

The project currently includes an interactive app prototype, a reusable design
system, and a tested household finance domain package. The backend has a process
health endpoint and an initial database connection layer.

Financial accounts, saved transactions, authentication, and household membership
are **not connected in the app yet**. Financial figures in previews are sample
data. This documentation describes both working foundations and planned
behavior, and calls out the difference.

## The direction

Quilt is mobile first, with a web companion. The current editing promise is
online editing with cached viewing, rather than a durable offline financial
write queue. Owners, editors, and viewers have distinct roles in a shared
workspace, and accounts retain their original currencies.

Those behaviors are being designed into the model. Cached viewing and backend
membership enforcement are not yet wired into the app.
